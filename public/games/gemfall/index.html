GEMFALL

Dig deep. Grab the crystals. Never stop under a boulder.
World 1 · Emberstone Caverns

How to play

  • Dig through soil. Walk into it to clear a path.
  • Collect crystals. Enough of them open the gate at the end of the cave.
  • Boulders fall. A falling boulder crushes you, so keep moving when one drops. Walk into a boulder twice to push it sideways.
  • Spikes and snakes hurt. Spikes rise on a beat. Drop a boulder on a snake and it leaves a crystal.
  • Use the cave. Hammers smash cracked walls. Boulders fill holes and hold pressure plates down. Crumbling floor breaks once you step off it.
  • Keys open doors. Lit braziers are checkpoints. You have 3 lives.
  • Find the ember crystal. Each level hides one.
